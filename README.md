<p>Lunchtime Jobs adds professions to Minecraft. You earn job XP by doing related activities, level up each profession and work toward its unlocks.</p>

<h3>Professions</h3>

<p><strong>Farmer</strong> — earn progress from farming activities, including growing and harvesting crops.</p>

<p><strong>Rancher</strong> — earn progress from looking after animals and working with livestock.</p>

<p><strong>Chef</strong> — earn progress from cooking food. Vanilla cooking is supported; some Farmer's Delight and Kaleidoscope cooking-station integrations are still being worked on.</p>

<p>Six of the 23 planned professions have been started in the current development build. Farmer and Rancher are confirmed working. Chef currently works with vanilla cooking; Farmer's Delight and Kaleidoscope cooking-station support still needs work.</p>

<p>Other professions in the planned roster include Bartender, Winemaker, Miner and Lumberjack. I'll add their details here as their gameplay is ready.</p>

<h3>How progression works</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Each profession has its own XP and level progression.</li>
  <li>XP comes from activities related to that job.</li>
  <li>Levels unlock profession rewards and perks as you progress.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Install</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Use Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge.</li>
  <li>Download Lunchtime Jobs from <a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/miso-jobs-careers">CurseForge</a>.</li>
  <li>Put the jar in your <code>mods</code> folder and launch the game.</li>
</ol>
