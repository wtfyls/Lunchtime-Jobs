<p>Ever wanted your Minecraft world to have a few actual jobs to work toward? Lunchtime Jobs adds professions that level up as you do related activities, with perks and progression to unlock along the way.</p>

<p>Pick the kind of work you enjoy, keep at it, and build up your profession over time. More jobs and progression features are being worked on.</p>

<h3>Install</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Install Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge.</li>
  <li>Download Lunchtime Jobs from <a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/miso-jobs-careers">CurseForge</a>.</li>
  <li>Put the jar in your <code>mods</code> folder and launch the game.</li>
</ol>
